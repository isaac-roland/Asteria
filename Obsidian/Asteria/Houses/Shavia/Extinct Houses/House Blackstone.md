House Blackstone was founded when the Southron King had a bastard named Valerius Swan. The Southron King fought against rebellion by the [[House Ravenstil|Ravenstils]], and he was bedridden with illness. Valerius was sent with his fathers army and defeated the Ravenstils in battle. He was made the head of his own house, Blackstone, named after the city he was born in. Then he was also granted the Duchy of Blackstone and made High Lord of the Province of Blackstone.
