With the capital of Anhelm, the Duchy of Blackstone had its capital changed in 1085 from the City of Blackstone to Anhelm. It is now ruled by the [[House Altherion]] of Anhelm. It was the personal holdings of the [[House Blackstone]] and once the [[Province of Blackstone]] was inherited by the Kontherns, they changed the Ducal capital from Blackstone, to Anhelm.
