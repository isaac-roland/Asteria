# The Stonefather:

### Creator of the Mountains: 

According to Children of Stone mythology, the Stonefather formed the mountains from the earth's primordial essence, shaping them with his hands and breathing life into them. As such, the mountains are believed to be his greatest creations, imbued with his divine power and essence.

  

### Guardian of the Land: 

The Stonefather is revered as the protector and guardian of the land, watching over the mountains and the people who dwell within them. He is believed to possess boundless strength and wisdom, capable of guiding his children through the trials and tribulations of life.

  

### Source of Wisdom: 

Followers of the Children of Stone believe that the Stonefather imparts his wisdom and guidance to those who seek his counsel. They believe that by communing with the mountains and honoring the Stonefather, they can gain insight and clarity in their lives.

  

### Eternal Presence: 

The Stonefather is seen as a timeless and eternal being, whose presence endures throughout the ages. Followers believe that he watches over them from the peaks of the highest mountains, his gaze ever-vigilant and his love unwavering.

# The Sleeping Children:

### Mountain Spirits: 

In Children of Stone mythology, the mountains are seen as the Stonefather's children, born from the earth and imbued with his divine essence. They are believed to possess their own spirits and personalities, with each mountain range having its own unique character and identity.

  

### The Sleeping Guardians: 

The mountains are often referred to as the "Sleeping Guardians," symbolizing their role as protectors of the land and its inhabitants. Followers believe that the mountains are not dormant entities but rather vigilant sentinels, watching over the land even as they slumber.

  

### Sacred Sites: 

Certain mountains are considered particularly sacred within Children of Stone belief, believed to be the resting places of the Stonefather's most beloved children. These mountains are revered as places of power and spiritual significance, where followers can commune with the divine and seek guidance from the Stonefather.

  

### Cycle of Awakening: 

Followers of the Children of Stone believe in the concept of the "Cycle of Awakening," wherein the mountains periodically awaken from their slumber to renew the land and bestow blessings upon its inhabitants. These awakenings are celebrated with festivals and rituals, marking the cyclical nature of life and the eternal bond between the Stonefather and his children.

  

# Leadership and Structure:

The Children of Stone may be led by a council of elders or spiritual leaders known as "Mountain Seers," who are revered for their wisdom, knowledge of the mountains, and connection to the divine. These Seers guide the religious practices of the community and serve as intermediaries between the mortal realm and the spiritual realm. With the exception of Mountain Seers, the religion remains decentralized.

# Traditions and Practices:

### Stone carving Artistry:

Stonecarving is not only a practical skill but also a deeply spiritual practice among the Children of Stone. It's believed that by carving symbols, reliefs, and sculptures into stone, they're not only honoring the earth but also imbuing the stone with spiritual energy and significance.

  

### Mountain Pilgrimages: 

Pilgrimages to sacred mountain peaks, deep caves, and ancient groves are central to the spiritual life of the Children of Stone. These journeys are undertaken as acts of reverence and devotion, providing opportunities for reflection, meditation, and communion with the divine essence of the land.

  

### Offerings to the Stonefather: 

Offerings of stones, crystals, and other natural artifacts are made to the Stonefather, the revered deity of the mountains. These offerings are placed at sacred sites or altars within the mountains as tokens of respect, gratitude, and devotion.

  

### Mountain Ceremonies: 

Ceremonies and rituals are held to honor the changing seasons, celestial events, and significant milestones in the lives of the Children of Stone. These ceremonies often involve chanting, drumming, and dancing, as well as the lighting of ceremonial fires and the offering of prayers to the Stonefather.

  

### Guardianship of the Land: 

The Children of Stone see themselves as stewards of the land, entrusted with its care and preservation. They actively try to protect their land, being hesitant to chop down forests or destroy mountains. 

  

### Ancestral Communion: 

Ancestral reverence is an integral part of the religious practices of the Children of Stone. Ancestral spirits are believed to dwell within the mountains, guiding and protecting their descendants. Rituals and ceremonies are performed to honor the ancestors and seek their wisdom and blessings.

  

### Stone Circles and Cairns: 

Stone circles and cairns are constructed at significant locations within the mountains as markers of spiritual significance and places of gathering for religious ceremonies and rituals. These stone structures are believed to amplify the spiritual energy of the land and facilitate communion with the divine.

  

### Sculptural Gardens: 

Gardens filled with sculpted stones, intricate mosaics, and symbolic artwork are cultivated as places of contemplation and meditation. These sculptural gardens serve as outdoor sanctuaries where followers can connect with the earth and find solace in its natural beauty.

  

### Seasonal Festivals: 

The Children of Stone celebrate a variety of seasonal festivals and holy days, marking the cycles of nature and the changing of the seasons. These festivals often involve communal feasting, storytelling, music, and dance, as well as rituals and ceremonies to honor the earth and the Stonefather.

  

### Stone Oracle Readings: 

Mountain Seers within the community perform stone oracle readings, using specially selected stones or crystals to gain insight into spiritual matters, seek guidance from the ancestors, and interpret the will of the Stonefather. Prophecy says there will one day be a single Stone Oracle, who will guide their people to prosperity. 

# Prophecy of the Stone Oracle:

### The Chosen One: 

The prophecy of the Stone Oracle foretells the coming of a chosen individual who will serve as a conduit between the Stonefather and his children. This individual, known as the Stone Oracle, is believed to possess the ability to commune with the divine and unlock the hidden secrets of the mountains.

  

### Awakening the Stone Dragons: 

It is said that the Stone Oracle will awaken the stone dragons that dwell within the caves of the mountains, unleashing their power and wisdom upon the world. These stone dragons are believed to be ancient guardians of the mountains, endowed with great knowledge and insight.

  

### Fulfilling the Prophecy: 

The coming of the Stone Oracle is eagerly awaited by the followers of the Children of Stone, who believe that their arrival will herald a new era of enlightenment and prosperity for their people. The Stone Oracle is seen as a savior figure, destined to fulfill the prophecy and usher in a golden age for the mountains and their inhabitants.

# The Mountains:

### Vulridin - 

The Sentinel of Family Bonds: Vulridin, known as The Sentinel of Family Bonds, stands tall as the embodiment of familial strength and unity. Pilgrims who undertake the journey to Vulridin seek to honor and protect their loved ones, drawing upon the mountain's steadfast presence to fortify the bonds of kinship. For those whose values are rooted in family ties and loyalty, Vulridin offers a sacred space where the ancestral spirits of the mountains watch over their descendants, guiding them in times of need and celebrating the enduring legacy of familial love.

### Ardor - 

The Hearth of Faith: Ardor's Summit, known as The Hearth of Faith, stands as a beacon of spiritual devotion and unwavering belief. Rising proudly above verdant valleys and rolling hills, Ardor's Summit symbolizes the fervent dedication and steadfast commitment of the faithful. Pilgrims who ascend Ardor's Summit do so with hearts ablaze with the flame of faith, seeking to deepen their connection to the divine and strengthen their spiritual resolve. Within the shadow of Ardor's Summit, the flames of devotion burn bright, illuminating the path of the faithful and guiding them towards enlightenment and salvation.

### Hallbrog - 

The Bastion of Honor and Justice: Hallbrog, revered as The Bastion of Honor and Justice, serves as a beacon of righteousness and integrity for those who seek to uphold the principles of fairness and moral rectitude. Perched upon craggy cliffs and overlooking sweeping vistas, this mountain stands as a symbol of unwavering resolve and unwavering commitment to truth and righteousness. Pilgrims who make the arduous ascent to Hallbrog do so with hearts ablaze with the fire of justice, pledging to defend the innocent and uphold the laws of the land. Within the shadow of Hallbrog, honor is not just a virtue but a sacred duty, and justice flows like a mighty river, nourishing the land with its righteous decree.

### Aldermore - 

The Cradle of Wisdom: Aldermore, known as The Cradle of Wisdom, is a mountain revered by seekers of knowledge and enlightenment. Nestled amidst serene forests and cascading waterfalls, Aldermore is a place of quiet contemplation and scholarly pursuits. Pilgrims who journey to Aldermore seek to expand their minds and deepen their understanding of the world, drawing upon the mountain's ancient wisdom to unlock the secrets of the universe. Within the shadow of Aldermore, the pursuit of knowledge is not just a scholarly endeavor but a sacred quest for truth and enlightenment.

### Sylvestre - 

The Sanctuary of Harmony: Sylvestre Summit, known as The Sanctuary of Harmony, is a mountain revered for its tranquil beauty and serene atmosphere. Surrounded by lush forests and pristine lakes, Sylvestre Summit is a place of peace and balance, where pilgrims come to find solace and inner harmony. Those who undertake the journey to Sylvestre Summit seek to reconnect with nature and restore balance to their lives, drawing strength from the mountain's gentle embrace and the soothing whispers of the wind. Within the shadow of Sylvestre Summit, the chaos of the world fades away, and individuals find themselves enveloped in a sense of serenity and oneness with the natural world.
