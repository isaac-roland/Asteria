*Oh, the lass from High Hollow, her hair spun like gold,*  
*Eyes bright as the river, and a heart hard to hold.*  
*She’ll laugh as she loves you, then leave with the dawn,*  
*And you’ll wake up to find that your coin purse is gone!*

*So raise up your tankards, and drink while you may!*  
*For love’s but a shadow that won’t ever stay.*  
*She'll kiss you like summer, then vanish like spring,*  
*Oh, the lass from High Hollow, she don’t care a thing!*

*A lord and a beggar both fell to her charms,*  
*Each swearing she’d stay if he gave her his arms.*  
*But promises break like the waves on the shore,*  
*And she danced out the door with a wink—and no more!*
