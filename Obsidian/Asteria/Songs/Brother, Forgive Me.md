*I saw you stand with sword in hand,*  
*No longer brother, just a man.*  
*The banners flew, the ravens cried,*  
*One wing to soar, one cast aside.*

*The night was cold in Ravenmark,*  
*Steel flashed like fire in the dark.*  
*They called it fate, they called it war,*  
*But left our house forever torn.*

*Brother, forgive me, for I am one,*  
*The Source bear witness, to what I've done.*  
*I swore my oath, I held my line,*  
*And now your blood is spilt with mine.*
