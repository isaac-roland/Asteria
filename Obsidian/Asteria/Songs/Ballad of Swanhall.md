*Oh, ride ye bold knights, through valley and glen,*  
*With banners so bright and a hundred brave men.*  
*The King gave the order, the castle must fall,*  
*"Take Swanhall by sword, leave no mercy at all."*

*But Don, he stood still, with his hand on his blade,*  
*He gazed at the tower where a fair maiden stayed.*  
*Her hair shone like sunlight, her voice like a tune,*  
*"No blood shall be spilled ‘neath the light of the moon."*

*He climbed not the walls, nor shattered the gate,*  
*But knelt at her feet and surrendered to fate.*  
*She took up his hand, and she whispered so low,*  
*"A heart won by love is a heart you must show."*

*The siege it was broken, not by fire or steel,*  
*But the vow of a knight and the love he made real.*  
*No blade drew a breath, no banners were torn,*  
*And Swanhall still stands where true love was sworn.*
