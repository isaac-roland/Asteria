Before House Gallo ruled Freystarde, the Embyrs held the territory for centuries. Felix Embyr was a lieutenant of Elyon (Some claim that he only served one of his descendants) while he spread his teachings. The story goes that as a reward for defending Elyon's disciples, Felix was granted a fief upon the sea. Elyon entrusted him with leagues upon leagues of land, tasking the new Lord with defending the people who lived there and bringing them into the Light. The people here were cited as having skin "The color of brass", and "Charcoal-colored hair". Leading many to consider them ancestors to the Murlaykans today. 

While the teachings of Elyon spread, many of the traditions of these people either persisted or blended with Imperial customs. This granted them a unique position in the empire. One that gave them pride in their differences, but also a jealousy derived from the lack of equality. House Embyr pushed House Letum's wishes upon the people of Freystarde, always pointing out the differences in culture. 

Over the centuries, rebellions came and went. The only real change in the status quo was the skin color of the people being noted as "Olive". Most likely from some interbreeding with the rest of the empire. Eventually, various Murlaykan trade empires started emerging in the east. The people of Freystarde sometimes saw more in common with them than that of their own lords. Cassius IX Embyr's reign saw a massive uprising under him. He writes in his diary that "The Heathens hail from the east. "
