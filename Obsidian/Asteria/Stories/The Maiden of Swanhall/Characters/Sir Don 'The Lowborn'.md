Sir Don of Swanhall was a knight sent by the Southron King to take the city of Swanhall from Elenora Swan. In the story of [The Maiden of Swanhall](obsidian://open?vault=Asteria&file=Stories%2FThe%20Maiden%20of%20Swanhall%2FStory%20-%20The%20Maiden%20of%20Swanhall), he did not take the city and instead fell in love with Elenora Swan and married her. He declared himself the Guardian of Swanhall and lived out the rest of his days as Lord of Swanhall. 

He was said to have had 5 children, but their names are lost to history. 

Sir Don fought years against the Southron King, but did not manage to defeat him. Sir Don was assumably a follower of the Stonefather, but as we know House Konthern was one of the first to convert to the Path of Guiding Light. 

The name Konthern is assumed to come from the Stone Tongue where Sir Don's name was most likely "Kon" and the suffix "thern" translates to either "King", "Royal", or "Lord". Some argue it also means descendant. Thus, over the ages, Sir Don's descendants would add Konthern to the end of their titles, and historians take that to mean either "Descendant of Don" or "Descendant of Lord Don".
