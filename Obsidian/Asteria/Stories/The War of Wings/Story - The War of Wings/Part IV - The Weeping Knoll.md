**The War of Wings**

**Pt. IV**

The King's men slaughtered the Hearstons where they stood. It took only minutes, before the grey bricks of the throne room were stained red. Lucas Hearston and his men stood no chance. Velya Blackstone was swiftly thrown into house arrest and her small retainer was imprisoned. It was said that the King took many liberties with her from then on. Far off in Slutiarm, Gerit Hearston heard about these events. His eldest son brutally slain alongside his men, his wife imprisoned and likely abused, action needed to be done. 

And so he marched. Gerit Hearston formed an army famously called "Gerit's 10,000", in mere days. These men were hardened. They were veterans against the Murlaykans in their war 15 years earlier, and were unquestionably loyal to the Hearstons. Meanwhile, down south, the Blackstones and Ravenstils put together an army of 40,000 men and around 3,000 knights. Aldric Ravenstil's son, Aldron, stayed behind and worked on an alliance with the Kontherns. Aldric along with his other son Aldren led the Ravenstil and Blackstone force to battle. Unfortunately for them, blizzards and snow blocked the valley pass between Madinglia and them, so they had to go around if they wanted to meet up with the Hearston army. 

This is the time when the Carthens declared for Gareth (With, of course, a marriage). They took their fleet and sailed it as close to Madinglia as they could, and rode to take Gerit's army by surprise. And Gerit was shocked. Lord Mason Carthen snuck deep into Madinglia with ~15,000 men, and burnt holdfasts and villages to the ground. Gerit was forced to turn around and deal with them. Gerit was outnumbered, but knew his men were better fighters. These Carthen soldiers had likely never seen battle before, and Gerit wanted to exploit that. Gerit gathered some of his few cavalry and had them ride to the Carthen camp early in the morning. They feigned an attack, and so the Carthens woke up and prepared for battle. During this, Gerit's men slept. Mason Carthen prepared all of his men for battle, before the sun even rose, thinking that the Hearstons had stolen a march on them in the night. Little did he know, the Hearstons were still miles and miles away getting well-rested. 

Mason Carthen's men chased the cavalry with his own, drawing them away from the battle for now. That was one of the only advantages that the Carthens had, and Gerit stole it away. The Carthen scouts informed Mason that the Hearstons slept still, and so they marched to attack. The march took longer than Mason thought, through the rough Madinglian terrain. By the time he reached the Hearston camp they had already been fed and woken up. Gerit positioned his men on a hill, and his bowmen began firing. The Madinglian archers rained on the Carthen men, who thought they were ambushing them. Mason Carthen knew there was no chance of withstanding the archers, and could not retreat either. So, Lord Mason ordered a full charge up the hill. The Madinglians continued firing, and waited. When the Carthens finally got to them they had no chance. Not only were the Madinglians better fighters but Gerit had made sure that the Carthens were tired, unfed, and exhausted from climbing the hill. Not to mention the Shavian cavalry was taken out of the equation. The Carthens stood no chance, and the Madinglians broke them with ease. Gerit apparently sought out Mason Carthen himself, and his retainer captured him before he could retreat. They won the battle, and Gerit was able to turn his attention to the West.
