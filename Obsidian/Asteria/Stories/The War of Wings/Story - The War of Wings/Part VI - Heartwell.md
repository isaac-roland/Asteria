**The War of Wings**

**Pt. VI**

Gerit received word of the Bleeding Spring before the battle was finished. He had to act fast if he wanted even a chance of winning this war. Gerit's 10,000 men were outnumbered near 5 to 1, so he needed a way to make up the numbers. He sends out a small scout contingent, to draw Lord Hoster's host into a valley. Hoster figures that there is some sort of trap, and goes around. Gerit decides to flee into the mountains of Swan's Reach, where Hoster wants to trap him. Weeks go by as Gerit's men hide from Hoster's army in the barren mountains. They raid Hoster's supplies, and pick off whoever they can. Gerit's foe was worn down and tired. To try and trap them, he collapses rocks onto the road into the mountains. Snow begins to fall and Lord Hoster knows that the situation is devolving. Gerit's men descend upon the dwindling Princeps and Merek force. 

Gerit and his Hearston soldiers easily pushed Hoster's force into chaos. It's said that Lord Hoster fought a duel with Gerit, where a stray arrow caught him. It's speculated that some deception took place, causing word to spread through Lord Hoster's army that their commander was dead. The Princeps and Merek men fled, leading to Gerit declaring victory in the Battle in the Snow.

The Hearston host immediately raced towards the capital. The hope was that they could take the city before any defense could be mounted. Aron Merek apparently went back to Sirong following news of this defeat. King Gareth panicked, for his remaining army was only the City Guards led by an ancestor to Frederick Reynar. Within a week Gerit found himself in Heartwell. Lord Hearston did not wish to discuss terms. He would take this city not with words, but with fire and sword. Gerit had significantly less numbers than when his 10,000 marched from Madinglia. If he wanted to take the city he needed more men, or a good strategy. Men he didn't have, but strategy he had aplenty. Gerit elected that if he could get inside the city, they wouldn't stand a chance. The plan was to take the city, and hold out for reinforcements from the Blackstones and hopefully the Kontherns. 

Thus, Gerit gathered his force at one of the gates. Using ladders, they scaled the walls to take the gatehouse. The Hearston deaths numbered in the hundreds before the gates finally opened. Gerit and a small contingent on horseback rushed to the keep. He left one of his sons in charge of the fighting in the city in the meantime. Accounts of what happened outside the keep are unclear and disputed, but for some reason the gates were open. Gerit's men fought the city guardsmen as he rushed into the throne room. Waiting for him was his wife, Velya. She was hanging from the ceiling with a noose around her neck. The King Gareth Princeps sat on the throne holding a bloody knife, allowing blood to seep from his throat. 

Gerit wept, as his son joined him. He informed his father that a Merek contingent lead by Lord Protector Aron had come to bring peace to Heartwell. Gerit was also informed that House Blackstone was gone. Most of their house had gone to secure a marriage pact with the Kontherns, but sadly the boat sank. This left the only living Blackstone as being married to the Lord of Swanhall. Now the Kontherns had openly proclaimed loyalty to King Gareth and planned to secure the Blackstone vassals. 

In other words, they needed to leave. Gerit had two of his sons with him, Varin and Odran, aged twelve and fourteen. Varin, being the younger, was squiring for his older brother. Gerit commanded that they would retreat to Madinglia to defend to the last man The remaining force at this point numbered less than 4,000. Many had finally grown skeptical of Gerit's judgement after his wife had died. He had held on to her body so that he could bury her in Hearston lands, and his decision to retreat was seen as an emotional one.

Aron Merek chased after the dwindling army with great haste. For leagues upon leagues, Gerit's men ran. Those who could not keep up were left to capture. Hundreds died to the cold and to fatigue. At last, they made it into Madinglia. But this was when Gerit's reckoning came. Aron had finally caught up to him. Merek cavalry cut off the Hearston men still on the run, and forced them to turn and fight. Aron was no military leader, but he certainly had men who were. They surrounded the Hearston men in the woods, and closed in. Gerit ordered his son, Odran, to break the encirclement so he could flee to Slutiarm. Gerit elected to make his final stand, and buried his wife beneath his feet. 

Gerit and his most loyal soldiers fought off the Merek onslaught as long as they could. Meanwhile, Odran had broken through. He and his brother were making it out, but at the cost of Varin's life. The twelve year old Varin was run down by Merek cavalry, and bled out in Odran's arms. 

It is said that Gerit was the last of his men to die. He could have been brought to trial, but Aron would not have it. Gerit Hearston was hung as a traitor to The Crown, and left to rot.
