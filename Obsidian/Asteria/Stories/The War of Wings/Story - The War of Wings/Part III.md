**The War of Wings**

**Pt. III**

The Queen Valya Blackstone was grabbed by the crowd. Mistaken for Gerit's wife, the crowd stoned her and her newborn son to death. Chaos descended on the tourney grounds, as guards and knights alike battled the peasants. It took a full day for the situation to resolve, but by the time it did the damage was done. A total of 200 peasants and 50 guards/nobles were killed in what became known as the "Butchers Tourney". The King was furious. He blamed his friend and longtime counsellor Gerit Hearston for what happened, as Gerit's wife was the one who the crowd wanted. Not her twin sister, the Queen. A month of mourning followed, where the bells rang twice every morning for the deceased Queen, and her dead son. Many say that the King was retired for his chambers for much of that time, ignoring his children and his councilors. 

Once the month was over, Lord Protector Gerit Hearston was fired from the council. It remains unclear to this day if it was ordered by the King, who was still reportedly mourning, or Aron Merek. But it matters not. Gerit, though enraged, had no choice. He allegedly stormed into the King's chambers and shouted for an hour, before his wife Velya calmed him down. Around this time rumors started emerging around the King. ~~The Scholars say that the King would sneak through tunnels in the walls at night, and used them to watch Gerit's wife Velya while she slept.~~ (Erased from the books by King Wilson Princeps, later readded by Lord of Knowledge Ignisell Ardere). 

Less than a day after Gerit Hearston was removed from his position, Aron Merek was made Lord Protector of Shavia. Aron also removed Aldric Ravenstil from the council, citing old age. This was likely because Gerit had been friendly with Lord Ravenstil and was the one who appointed him. Aldric Ravenstil was the father of the twins Aldron and Aldren Ravenstil, whose death later caused the split into House Westraven and House Ravenstil. Also removed from the council, was Lord Vaxton Blackstone (Velya and Valya's father), and Lord Aldric's son Aldron. This fractured the alliance Gerit had worked to form between House Ravenstil, Blackstone, Hearston, and Princeps so many years ago.

Before leaving for Slutiarm, Gerit was informed that his wife Velya would stay behind and join the council as Lady of Laws. Gerit was outraged, but he didn't exactly have a choice. He went back to Slutiarm and began planning. He began mobilizing messengers, trying to foster alliances with his unmarried children. The Crown (Mainly Aron) responded in kind by ensuring that these alliances were formed with House Princeps or House Merek instead. In a confusing web of marriage pacts, the only one left unaligned was the Kontherns, who, much to the annoyance of both sides, retained their policy of eternal neutrality. 

The Kingdom waited in anticipation, as war looked more and more likely. Unlike 15 years prior, there was no Great Council called to resolve the matter. Instead, one event sparked the powder keg that was Shavia. Gerit knew his wife could not stay in Heartwell by herself, so he sent his firstborn son Lucas Hearston with 50 men to retrieve her. When he arrived, the King was outraged. In the throne room Lucas asked the King where his mother was, and said that she would be going back to Slutiarm. Before even Aron could stop him, the King shouted for "The heads of every Stone-Goblin in my city". Needless to say, he got what he wanted.
