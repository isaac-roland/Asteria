**The Split of House Ravenstil**

Aldric Ravenstil was the leader of House Ravenstil for decades leading up to the War of Wings. He gave birth to a pair of twins before the war, named Aldron and Aldren. In their childhood they got along as well as brothers could. They did everything together, training and being taught to lead equally. As they grew the issue of succession became more and more prevalent. His vassals had requested him to address it many times, though he neglected the issue. Keeping a faint hope that they might rule his dominion together after he died. A fool's dream. He kept his naive desire alive for years. Perhaps fueled by equal love for his two sons, but more likely came from a lack of conviction. 

Aldric Ravenstil became good friends with Gerit Hearston, an up and coming lord out of Madinglia. Gerit earned prestige during the Great Council of 1081, where he, (The youngly appointed Lord Protector) was credited with resolving the situation before it turned into a crisis. Gerit swayed Lord Aldric into siding with him, and supporting the young Gareth for the Crown over Prince Darian. That was when he helped arrange marriages with House Konthern and Blackstone, using Aldric's sons to construct these pacts. The Kontherns and Blackstones were separately told that their daughter would be married to the heir. Obviously a design of Gerit. 

These betrothals helped stave off the conflict and bought time for the council. Aldric mentored and appreciated the young Lord Protector, admiring his conviction in hunting down Prince Darian after he lost the vote. Spilling a few drops of blood to spare the lives of thousands. The Lordling Aldren married a Blackstone Lady, while Aldron married a Konthern. Before the war, Aldron had a son bearing the name Armin Ravensil. With this, came the vague expectation that Aldron would inherit Blackfeather Hall.

When the War of Wings approached, Aldric Ravenstil called his banners. He, alongside with his son Aldren, led the men of House Ravenstil and House Blackstone to fight against the alleged tyrant Gareth. Throughout the war Aldron (Married to a Konthern) was tasked with improving relations with House Konthern alongside the Blackstones. When a marriage was finally arranged, House Blackstone and their members attempted a voyage to Swanhall. Tragically, the ship struck a rock halfway into their journey. Nearly all of the members of House Blackstone perished, along with Aldron Ravenstil. 

Though, the eldest remaining Blackstone was already in Swanhall, preparing for the wedding. She wed the Lord Konthern and was left as the sole heir to the Blackstone lands, giving the Kontherns dominion over Blackfeather Hall. 

Aldric Ravenstil was slain in battle during the war. The Kontherns were left with deciding the fate of House Ravenstil, leaving it in the hands of the toddler Armin Ravenstil, or his uncle Aldren Ravenstil. Undoubtedly, the clearest choice was to grant the duchy to Aldren. He had the claim and capability. Though, Armin Ravenstil's mother was a Konthern. For the sake of keeping the peace (And perhaps control over the Ravenstils) Armin Ravenstil was made Lord of Ravenmark and of Blackfeather Hall. 

Ravenstil had some remaining men from the war, so Aldron rallied them. He took them to the western part of Ravenmark. There he created his own banner, changing the red in House Ravenstil's banner to blue. He declared his intent to retake Ravenmark, in both his name and his father's. Aldron accused House Konthern of empowering themselves by placing his nephew in charge, only so that a Konthern could serve as regent. He captured the seaside castle of Westle, and expected a siege by Lord Konthern. Time passed, but the attack never came. From Swanhall came a decree, placing the current dominion of Raven's Reach in the hands of Aldron Ravenstil. They would be ruled separately and have their own separate levies. The Kontherns even hung the new blue banner in their hall, beside the red Ravenstil one. 

Shortly after, Aldron said that he and his men would abide by their liege's declaration. But with the stipulation that he and his descendants would take the name 'Westraven'. No disagreement emerged. Thus, House Ravenstil and Westraven would diverge into two separate paths. Though these two branches had no love for one another. The next hundred years would be wrought with skirmishes and battles, where each side often chose to oppose the other whenever the opportunity arose.
