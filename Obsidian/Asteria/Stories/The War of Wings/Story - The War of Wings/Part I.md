**The War of Wings**

**Pt. I**
King Gareth 'The Grand' II Princeps ascended his throne in 1081. He and his Lord Protecter stood side by side, successful in stopping the collapse of Shavia. Lord Protector Gerit Hearston slew his King's uncle, Prince Darian, in dramatic fashion. Some might say dishonorably. Soon after, he took a liking to a woman. The Queen Valya Blackstone had a twin-sister named Velya. Valya was the prettier one, but Velya was the witty one. Gerit Hearston and Velya Blackstone fell in love, but did not marry. This brought the King Gareth and Lord Gerit closer together, as brothers. The King even commissioned a statue of his brother-in-law, to honor his dedication to the Crown and his efforts in preventing the civil war. 

King Gareth intended to forge the realm anew. He created an academy for scholars, trying to shape Heartwell into the shining light of knowledge and innovation. To spearhead this, the 20-year old Aron Merek sought out the King. Aron Merek was a cripple, and second son of the Lord of Sirong. Despite being a second son, he had ambition. He sought out the King, and championed that due to his lack of skill with a sword, he instead had skill with books. Aron seduced the King with flattery, and thus King Gareth created the council position the "Lord of Knowledge." Immediately, Gareth and Aron struck a liking to each other. Together they plotted fantastic things; a new city, just for academics. A Library, so grand that men around the world came to see. A battle of wits, to see which young men might yet become scholars.

Slowly, the King was captured in Aron's web. Aron began whispering in the King's ear during council meetings and when he sat on the throne. The King would open his mouth, and Aron's words would come out. The King and Lord Aron decided that they would build the library they had dreamed of. But where to build it? Aron knew. The statue the King had commissioned in honor of Lord Gerit was not finished, and was excessively large. This statue would not help the Kingdom, Aron said, but our library will. It needed to be torn down.
