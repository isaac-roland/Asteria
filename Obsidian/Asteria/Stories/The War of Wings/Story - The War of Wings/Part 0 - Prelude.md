Near 100 years prior to the start of our story, the realm stood at a crossroads. King Dilyn 'The Drunken'. King Dilyn held feasts and parties for his entire reign, letting his Lord Protector Gerit Hearston rule the realm for him. Gerit Hearston was appointed Lord Protector at the age of 18, as an apology for his father dying in the 1st Pearlpass war. This was an attempt to keep the Hearstons happy, as they had nearly refused to fight. The King's advisors told him not to put such a young man in charge of the realm, which the King ignored. Needless to say, King Dilyn had around 5 bastard children, and 6 legitimate sons. At this point King Dilyn had outlived all of his legitimate children, and the only ones left were his bastards. 

However, he had one grandchild, born of his youngest son and was only 14 at this time. The realm was weary of such a young King, paired with a Lord Protector that was hardly older. This 14 year old was Prince Gareth. Gareth was anything but a warrior; he studied with Scholars for his childhood while his uncles were all renowned swordsmen. The eldest of his bastard uncles was Prince Darian 'Gold', the Lord of Dreghold and a tourney knight who won every tournament he entered. The realm looked to him and saw a worthy King. Despite his advisor's pleadings, he never appointed an heir. The Blackstones married one of their girls to Prince Gareth, to solidify their support. One by one alliances were made, and Lords braced for war.

The King could hardly speak, and was bedridden at the end. Lord Gerit at this point had done his best to keep war from breaking out. Despite his efforts, his age was the only thing the Kingdom cared about. He knew something had to be done, and he called the Great Council of 1081. Lord Gerit declared that all the Lords of Shavia would put forth a vote as to who should be King. Many a Lord spoke their claim, but only 2 emerged as real candidates. Prince Darian and Prince Gareth. After weeks of voting, Prince Gareth emerged as the victor. 

Prince Darian had proclaimed his nephew a pretender in outrage, and meant to make himself King. However, Lord Gerit already knew that Darian would not comply with the results of the council, and before he could even flee Heartwell, Prince Darian was ambushed in the streets by him. His men were slain, but Darian fought on. He fought a magnificent duel with the young Lord Gerit. Legends say it was near an hour, but eventually Gerit prevailed. Darian should have been taken prisoner. However, Lord Gerit made the dishonorable, but logical choice to risk years of war. He sacrificed his own honor, to end a civil war. The realm exhaled a sigh of relief, and King Gareth II ascended the throne.
