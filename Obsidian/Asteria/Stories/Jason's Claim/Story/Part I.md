James II Princeps spent much of his 51 year reign revitalizing the broken realm. Nicknamed 'The Reforger' for his role in turning the Kingdom into one united behind House Princeps. He had much assistance from Aron Merek during the first few years of his reign, whereas the controversial lord served as Lord Protector and Regent for some time. Aron arranged the marriage of his nephew Pernos Merek to the King's daugher, Tella Princeps, creating an official tie between the two families. Aron died in 1120, and his final act was betrothing Marian Umbra to the King's son and heir, Jacob Princeps. 

James II was deeply popular. He, alongside House Konthern, kept the realm united until his death. It also helped that his younger brother, Jason Princeps, held Dreghold and ruled as its High Lord. Jacob Princeps was a mostly reclusive figure, and seen as uncharismatic. Primarily thought to be a result of his agnosticism on The Source. Upon The Reforger's death, this gave Jacob's uncle; Jason, an opening. 

Jason had brought his retainer to Heartwell, and flew his personal banner above the city. His claim was that his brother stated that his son would be passed over in the line of succession on account of his sacrilege. No one could corroborate this claim, as Jason said it was his final breath. 

Jacob Princeps was placed under house arrest, along with most of his immediate family. Jason 'The Usurper' Princeps ruled for barely over two years. Most of the noble houses of Shavia had been against his reign, and only supported it on the basis that he controlled Heartwell and had custody of the rest of the royal family. A coalition began to form, led by Pernos Merek. He had the backing of House Lora, House Carthen, House Umbra, and House Konthern. Pernos Merek already held the position of Lord Protector, and presented Jason Princeps with an ultimatum. Abdicate, and be exiled, or face the rebellion of much of Shavia. He chose the latter.

The Usurper was fooled, however. Pernos Merek used his influence to free Jason's captives. Jacob Princeps was freed and fled to Sirong, where he found himself at the helm of the coalition Pernos Merek formed. Jacob Princeps had been friends with his cousin, Luther Princeps, whom The Usurper had named Lord of Dreghold. His first move was to march to Dreghold and reason with his cousin. 

Luther Princeps had called his levies, and met Jacob on the field of battle. Jacob hoped that Luther would relent once they talked, and called for a parley. They talked for hours in a tent, far from either army. Luther emerged from the tent kneeling to Jacob Princeps. The news spread as a shock to many. Particularly Luther's father, Jason. Together the two men marched on Heartwell, placing the city under siege. It lasted for months. Pernos Merek wanted to storm the city, and Luther was in agreement. However, Jacob was reluctant to take Heartwell by force for two reasons. If he was to be King, these would be his people. Jacob had no desire for them to feel conquered. Moreover, they still held hostages. Particularly his mother. The Lord Alton Konthern would not see his daughter (The Queen Mother) dead under any circumstance. 

They agreed to give Jacob one attempt. A small force scaled the walls in the night, with the goal of releasing the remaining hostages. So he did. He, Pernos Merek, and fifty others rowed into the city from upriver. They took a smugglers route that Aron Merek had apparently shown to his nephew before pasing, and got into the city. Unfortunately, upon reaching the keep, the guards were alerted. Jacob and Pernos fought their way through the keep, before getting to the dungeons. Them and the hostages found themselves in the throne room, where dozens of guards cornered them. It's said that Jacob spoke to them,"

"Do you remember me? I am your rightful King, Jacob Princeps. My father was James II, the Reforger. Yes, I know your faces. We trained together in that yard out there. We grew up together, bled together. And if we had gone to war, I would have considered it an honor to die together. 

"I bid you now, remember the vow you swore my father. He still lives on in me. Kneel to me, and I will continue his legacy. If his memory has already passed from this earth, then do what you came here to do. I would be honored to die in his name,"

It's said that one by one, the men kneeled. Jacob Princeps walked up the steps to the throne, and took his place. The entire city surrendered within an hour, and Jason was brought before the new King. His punishment was to be exiled indefinitely, which lasted until his death 4 years later. Luther Princeps was sentenced with "Protecting Dreghold" until Wilson Princeps (The King's newborn son) should come of age. Luther Princeps fled the realm soon after for reasons unknown. He would marry a woman from Freystarde, and have a son; Marcel. He would die away from Shavia, but his wife Chloe brought the young Marcel to Heartwell, where he grew and married a Ravenstil. 

Jacob Princeps would die at 60, having been named 'Bearclaw' for his ferocity and personification of House Princeps. The friendship of Pernos and Jacob led to Pernos' son, Philson Merek, and Jacob's firstborn, and future King; Wilson Princeps being fostered together. They would be great friends, and Philson Merek became Lord Protector upon his father's passing.
